# store-annual-sales-report-excel
Interactive Excel dashboard using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts and Slicers to analyze annual store sales by channel, gender, age group, state and order status.

##  Objective
To turn raw sales data into clear insights that answer:
- How do orders and sales change month by month?
- Who buys more: men or women?
- Which age group contributes the most sales?
- Which states and sales channels perform best?
- What share of orders are delivered, returned, cancelled or refunded?

##  Dataset
- Sheet: **Vrinda Store** (raw sales data)
- Fields include: Order ID, Date, Month, Channel, Category, Gender, Age Group, State, Order Status, Amount, Qty


##  Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning (removing duplicates, fixing formats, handling blanks)
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Slicers (Channel, Category, Months)
- Dashboard design and formatting

## Dashboard Visuals
- **Order vs Sales**: combo chart of monthly sales (columns) and orders (line)
- **Sales: Men vs Women**: gender share of sales
- **Order Status**: delivered, returned, cancelled, refunded
- **Sales: Top 5 States**: highest-selling states
- **Sales: Order vs Age Group**: sales split by age group and gender
- **Orders: Channels**: order share by channel
- **Slicers**: filter by Channel, Category and Month

## Key Insights
- Women contribute about 64% of sales, while men contribute about 36%.
- Adults (about 52% women, 23% men) are the biggest buying group, followed by seniors and teenagers.
- Amazon (35%), Myntra (23%) and Flipkart (22%) together account for about 80% of orders.
- About 92% of orders are delivered, and returns, cancellations and refunds together are about 8%.
- Maharashtra and Karnataka are the top states by sales.
- Sales and orders peak around March and decline toward the end of the year.
- Recommendation: focus marketing on women aged 25-45 (adults) and on the top 3 channels.

## How to Use
1. Download `Store Data Analysis.xlsx`.
2. Open it in Excel and go to the **Sales Report** sheet.
3. Use the Channel, Category and Months slicers to filter the charts.
